Project 01

MOSA Medical Camp

An annual medical outreach camp organised by Mengo Hospital doctors, nurses, and health professionals providing the Mengo community with affrodable testing services

Annual Event Active

About the Camp

The MOSA Medical Camp is a flagship community service initiative where old students with parternship with medical professionals doctors, nurses from Mengo Hospital provide their services and expertise to provide healthcare services to the school community of Mengo Senior School.

The camp provides general consultations, blood pressure and diabetes screening, dental check-ups, eye testing, immunisations, and free medication distribution. Beyond treatment, we run health education sessions on nutrition, maternal health, mental wellness, and hygiene.

Every year the camp grows in scale from a single-day event to a multi-day outreach touching hundreds of lives. It is funded through MOSA donations, member contributions, and corporate sponsorships from Mengonian-owned businesses.

Services Offered

General Consultations

Consultations with qualified doctors for all ages.

Eye Testing

Vision screening and referrals for corrective lenses.

Dental Check-ups

Oral hygiene education and basic dental care.

Health Screening

Blood pressure, blood sugar, and BMI checks for all.

Project 02

Mengonian Excellence Scholarship

Funding 10 financially disadvantaged students annually through their O-Level and A-Level studies at Mengo Senior School.

Active Applications Open

What the Scholarship Covers

The Mengonian Scholarship was born from a simple belief: no child should be denied a Mengo education because of money. Alumni pool resources annually to identify and fully fund students who lack financial means.

School Fees
Full tuition covered
Scholastic Materials
Books, stationery, uniforms
Mentorship
Assigned MOSA mentor

Eligibility Criteria

  • Admitted or currently enrolled at Mengo Senior School
  • Demonstrated financial hardship (verified by school bursar)
  • Strong academic record minimum aggregate 6 (PLE) or 5As (UCE)
  • Recommendation from class teacher or headteacher
  • Commitment to community service and giving back

Applications submitted via the school administration
