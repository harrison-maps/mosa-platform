Project 02

Mengonian Excellence Scholarship

Funding 10 financially disadvantaged students annually through their O-Level and A-Level studies at Mengo Senior School.

Active Applications Open

What the Scholarship Covers

The Mengonian Scholarship was born from a simple belief: no child should be denied a Mengo education because of money. Alumni pool resources annually to identify and fully fund students who lack financial means.

School Fees
Full tuition covered
Scholastic Materials
Books, stationery, uniforms
Mentorship
Assigned MOSA mentor

Eligibility Criteria

  • Admitted or currently enrolled at Mengo Senior School
  • Demonstrated financial hardship (verified by school bursar)
  • Strong academic record minimum aggregate 6 (PLE) or 5As (UCE)
  • Recommendation from class teacher or headteacher
  • Commitment to community service and giving back

Applications submitted via the school administration

Scholarship Calendar

Jan

Applications Open

School identifies and nominates eligible candidates. Forms distributed through school administration.

Mar

MOSA Selection Panel

A committee of MOSA members reviews applications and conducts brief interviews. 10 scholars selected.

Apr

Scholarship Awarded

Scholars announced at the MOSA Annual Gala. Fees paid directly to the school. Mentors assigned.

Dec

Annual Review

Scholars present their progress. Renewal decided based on performance and continued need.
