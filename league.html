What Is the Mengo Elite League?

The Mengo Elite League (MEL) is an idea conceived in 2019 by a group of Mengo old students with the goal of bringing together and strengthening lost ties through sports specifically football (soccer).

Its core aim is to uphold Mengo Senior School's values through sports, networking, and making a difference in people's lives through engagement in life-changing activities. We recognised that many had lost touch with colleagues due to busy schedules, and the league was born to bridge that gap.

The league uses a 7-a-side format (6 gentlemen + 1 lady per team) played at Mengo Senior School's official pitch, with teams organised by graduation year, all the way through to group stages and a grand final.

Sports & Fitness

Improving health and fitness of old students through active participation in football.

Networking

Bridging the gap between old students across different graduation years through shared passion.

Gender Inclusion

Every team must include at least 1 lady ensuring equal opportunity for all Mengonians.

Giving Back

League funds contribute to community and school development through corporate social responsibility.

The League Structure

How the MEL Works

1

Form Your Team

Gather 7 players, 6 gentlemen and at least 1 lady, ideally from the same graduation year. Pay the team registration fee.

2

Group Stage

All teams compete in group stages at Mengo Senior School's pitch, with qualified referees and linesmen officiating.

3

Knockout Rounds

Top teams advance through knockout rounds. League rules are strictly enforced violations attract penalties.

Grand Final

Champions are crowned at the Grand Final. The winning team earns the MEL title and the season prize pool.
