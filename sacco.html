The Concept

What Is the MOSA SACCO?

The MOSA SACCO is a member-owned Savings and Credit Cooperative Organisation open exclusively to Mengo Old Students. Members make regular monthly contributions into a shared fund, which earns interest and is available as affordable loans.

Unlike a bank, your SACCO works entirely for you. Every shilling saved stays within the Mengonian community, generating returns that benefit all members. It is governed by elected Mengonians with full transparency and accountability.

Whether you want to grow your savings, fund your business, pay school fees, or simply build a financial safety net — the MOSA SACCO is your most trusted partner.
