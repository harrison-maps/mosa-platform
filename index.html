Our Legacy & Mission

Mengo Old Students Association (MOSA) is a prestigious global network of alumni dedicated to preserving the rich heritage of Mengo Senior School. We exist to foster lifelong connections among former students, promote professional excellence, and actively give back to ensure our alma mater remains a beacon of premier education in Uganda and beyond.
