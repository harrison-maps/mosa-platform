Who We Are

Our Legacy & Mission

Mengo Old Students Association (MOSA) is a prestigious global network of alumni dedicated to preserving the rich heritage of Mengo Senior School. We exist to foster lifelong connections, celebrate excellence, and invest in the institution that shaped us all.

Dr. Mulwana James

Industrialist & Businessman

One of Uganda's most celebrated industrialists and a proud product of Mengo Senior School, Dr. Mulwana James built a business empire spanning manufacturing, finance, and real estate leaving a lasting legacy on Uganda's economy.

Hon.Justice Steven kavuma

Former Deputy Chief Justice · Court of Appeal

A distinguished jurist and former Deputy Chief Justice of Uganda's Court of Appeal, Hon. Steven Kavuma has spent decades upholding justice with integrity embodying the leadership and character that Mengo Senior School has cultivated for generations.
