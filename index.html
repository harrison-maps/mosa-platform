Our Legacy & Mission

Mengo Old Students Association (MOSA) is a prestigious global network of alumni dedicated to preserving the rich heritage of Mengo Senior School. We exist to foster lifelong connection.

Joseph Mayanja

Musical Phenomenon

Kampala, Uganda

Known across Uganda and East Africa as one of the most gifted musicians of his generation, Joseph Mayanja known as Jose Chameleone is a global ambassador of Ugandan music and a proud Mengonian whose artistry transcends borders.
