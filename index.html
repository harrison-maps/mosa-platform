Who We Are

Our Legacy & Mission

Mengo Old Students Association (MOSA) is a global network of alumni dedicated to preserving the heritage of Mengo Senior School. We exist to foster lifelong connections, celebrate excellence, and invest in the institution that shaped us all.

Dr. Mulwana James

Industrialist & Businessman

One of Uganda's most celebrated industrialists and a proud product of Mengo Senior School, Dr. Mulwana James built a business empire spanning manufacturing, finance, and real estate — leaving a lasting legacy on Uganda's economy.

Hon. Justice Steven Kavuma

Former Deputy Chief Justice · Court of Appeal

A distinguished jurist and former Deputy Chief Justice of Uganda's Court of Appeal, embodying the leadership and character that Mengo Senior School has cultivated for generations.

Prof. Senteza Kajubi

Educator

Former Vice Chancellor of Makerere University and Nkumba University, and a profound educator.

Canon John Fred Kazibwe

Prominent Educator

A prominent Ugandan educator and lay canon, serving as the 14th headmaster of King's College Budo from November 2021 until March 2026 — following 10 years as headmaster of Mengo Senior School.
