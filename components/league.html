The Concept

What Is the MOSA Elite League?

The MOSA Elite League is a unique competitive platform where Mengonian business owners form teams, pay a membership fee to participate, and bring their businesses into a structured league environment — earning visibility, community support, and bragging rights.

Think of it as part business expo, part sports league, part community investment. Teams compete across seasons in categories like fastest-growing business, most community-impacting venture, and best Mengonian collaboration.

The membership fees collected go back into the MOSA ecosystem — funding events, scholarships, and school development — while your business gets unmatched exposure to 12,000+ alumni across Uganda and the world.

Compete

Go head to head with fellow Mengonian businesses across multiple seasonal categories.

Showcase

Your business gets a dedicated profile page seen by every MOSA member and visitor.

Connect

Build lasting partnerships with other team owners and the broader MOSA community.

Grow

Win customers, mentorship, and investment opportunities from within the Mengonian network.

The League Structure

How a Season Works

1

Registration

Teams register, pick a tier, and pay their seasonal membership fee. Your business profile goes live immediately.

2

Season Launch

An opening gala event launches the season. Teams pitch their businesses to the MOSA community and compete in round one.

3

Monthly Fixtures

Monthly networking and business challenge events. Community votes and judge scores decide the league table standings.

Grand Finale

Season ends with the Elite League Awards night. Champions lift the trophy and win the prize pool and exclusive MOSA benefits.
