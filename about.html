About MOSA

Our Mission

The Mengo Old Students Association (MOSA) exists to foster a lifelong and mutually beneficial relationship between Mengo Senior School and its alumni. We are dedicated to promoting professional excellence, creating networking opportunities, and mobilizing resources to support the continuous growth of our beloved alma mater.

Our Vision

To be the most vibrant, impactful, and globally connected alumni association in Uganda, empowering our members and ensuring Mengo Senior School remains a premier institution of learning.
