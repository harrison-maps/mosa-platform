Our Mission

The Mengo Old Students Association (MOSA) exists to foster a lifelong and mutually beneficial relationship between Mengo Senior School and its alumni. We are dedicated to promoting professional and social standards; supporting the school in its educational mission; and creating opportunities for alumni to reconnect, network, and contribute to society.

Our Vision

To be the most vibrant, impactful, and globally connected alumni association in Uganda, empowering our members and ensuring Mengo Senior School remains a premier institution of learning, character building, and leadership.

Financial Empowerment for Mengonians

MOSA SACCO

The MOSA Savings and Credit Cooperative Organisation (SACCO) is the financial home of Mengo Old Students. Together, we save, grow, and support each other — because a Mengonian's success is our collective success.

What Is the MOSA SACCO?

The MOSA SACCO is a member-owned savings and credit group exclusively for Mengo Old Students. Members make regular contributions into a shared pool, which then earns interest and can be borrowed from at affordable rates.

Unlike a bank, your SACCO works for you. Every shilling you save stays within the Mengonian community, generating returns that benefit all members. It is run by elected Mengonians, with full transparency and accountability.
