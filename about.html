About MOSA

Preserving the legacy of Mengo Senior School and uniting generations of excellence since 1895.

Our Mission

The Mengo Old Students Association (MOSA) exists to foster a lifelong and mutually beneficial relationship between Mengo Senior School and its alumni. We are dedicated to promoting professional and social standards; supporting the school in its educational mission; and creating opportunities for alumni to reconnect, network, and contribute to society.

Our Vision

To be the most vibrant, impactful, and globally connected alumni association in Uganda, empowering our members and ensuring Mengo Senior School remains a premier institution of learning, character building, and leadership.
